What we don't say about Moral Argument?

What we don't say about Moral Argument? We are not saying morality has to be objective, there for our God. No we don't say that. it's not to prove any specific theistic God. There are three religions that believe in a theistic God; Judaism, Christianity and Islam. So why do we use that argument in the first place? to show people who say "their is no God" you can't have a objective morality, even though it's seem like murder, raping etc wrong to us. If you reject the transcendent God your feeling or inclination to say some evil act wrong actually doesn't hold any ground. They can still reject God and say all of the ridiculously wrong things that happen in this world are not inherently wrong and it's just subjective. If it's something they are willing to hold, that's fine. But as a Christina we have the full right to say these things are objectively wrong no matter who ever does them because our God allows us to say that since those things ground and come from God.

If people wanna explain away and say "it's all subjective", we can urge them to think about those things. Deep down they know it's not right to murder somebody; but if they reject the marker, subjectivity is the thing they are left with.

Key points before finish the article

  1. I'm not saying morality is subjective.
  2. I'm also not saying people can't say murder is wrong.
  3. I'm saying if they don't base their morality on God, they end up failing to give an account for these evil things that happen in this world.
